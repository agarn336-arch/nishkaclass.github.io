RECIPE: MAKE A PLAYLIST

A simple recipe for creating a playlist that actually feels like you.

Ingredients

  • 3 songs you already love
  • 1 song you've never heard
  • 1 song recommended by someone else
  • 1 song connected to a memory
  • Headphones
  • 10 minutes

Instructions

  1. Choose 3 songs you could listen to forever.
  2. Ask someone to recommend a song you've never heard.
  3. Add one song that reminds you of a specific memory.
  4. Find one completely new song.
  5. Put all 6 songs into one playlist.
  6. Listen to the playlist from beginning to end without skipping.
  7. Give your playlist a name based on how it made you feel.
